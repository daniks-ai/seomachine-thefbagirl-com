# Daniks.AI — landing copy for Brazil (pt-BR) + Mexico (es-MX)

**Purpose.** thefbagirl.com now funnels Amazon sellers from `/br/` and `/mx/` to daniks.ai. Today those CTAs land on the English daniks.ai site — a language dead-end that leaks conversions. This doc is ready-to-implement landing copy in Brazilian Portuguese and Mexican Spanish so a lead who clicks in-language stays in-language.

**Where the traffic comes from.** CTAs on thefbagirl.com use `getCompanyUrl()` which appends UTM params (e.g. `utm_source=thefbagirl&utm_medium=blog&utm_campaign=daniks-hub-br`). Route pt-BR traffic (`utm_campaign=*-br`, or `Accept-Language: pt`) to the pt-BR landing, es-MX traffic (`*-mx` / `es`) to the es-MX landing. Preserve UTMs through to signup so GA4 on daniks.ai attributes the lead.

**Positioning (ground truth — keep consistent with `/br/daniks` and `/mx/daniks`).** AI-native Amazon PPC automation. You set a target ACoS; an AI agent handles bids, keywords, and negatives 24/7. Seller-built: Ekaterina Rubtcova built it for her own Daniks cookware brand (Top-1 in Germany, Top-20 in the USA) and it now runs hundreds of other Amazon brands. Works across Amazon marketplaces **including Amazon Brazil and Amazon Mexico**. Not a dashboard you babysit — an agent that makes the boring PPC decisions for you.

> ⚠️ **Verify before publishing:** exact price, trial length/SKU cap, and marketplace availability wording. Draft uses: 14-day free trial, up to 100 SKUs, no credit-card games, cancel anytime. Price shown as a placeholder (`US$ XX/mês`) — confirm the current BR/MX pricing.
