This is NOT an Instantly mass campaign. ~50-100 named accounts, sent manually
(or via a tiny dedicated Instantly campaign with 1:1 personalization), 2-3
touches max. Every email must contain at least one line that could only have
been written about THAT company (recent acquisition, portfolio brand, job
posting, podcast appearance).

Offer: **Agency plan / volume pricing + side-by-side A/B test** against their
current in-house process. NOT the 25% affiliate pitch — these companies ARE the
customer.

Persona priority (check LinkedIn/jobs page per account):
1. Head of Marketplace / Director of Amazon
2. VP Growth / VP E-commerce
3. Director of Advertising / PPC Lead
4. COO (smaller holdcos)

Economics framing that lands with portfolio operators:
- One PPC manager runs 5-8 accounts well; portfolios have 20-100+.
- Daniks.AI runs every account to its target ACoS 24/7 — campaigns, bids,
  keywords, negatives — so one operator supervises instead of executes.
- Built-in A/B test = zero-risk pilot on 2-3 brands against their current
  process; their own dashboards judge the result.
- $50M+ ad spend under management, 1,000+ sellers, built for our own FBA
  brands first (Daniks cookware), verified in the Amazon Ads Partner Directory.
