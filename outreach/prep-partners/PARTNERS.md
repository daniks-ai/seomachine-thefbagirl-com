# Prep-center partner registry + referral process

Backbone of the two-way deal promised in `sequences/prep-centers-en.md`:
partners refer clients to Daniks.AI (25% lifetime), we refer sellers who ask
for prep services back to partners. This file is the single source of truth
for who is a partner and who got which lead.

## Process (option A — manual intros)

1. A seller asks support/Alex/Katia "can you recommend a prep center?"
2. Pick 1–2 partners from the table below matching the seller's region
   (state for US, country otherwise) and needs (OA/wholesale/hazmat/FBM).
3. Send the intro email (template below) with BOTH the seller and the
   partner in To/Cc — the visible intro IS our proof the promise is kept.
4. Log the intro in the "Leads passed" table.

Priority when several partners match: fewest leads received so far, then
oldest signup date — keep distribution fair, nobody stays at zero.

## Intro email template

> Subject: Intro: {seller first name} ({brand}) ↔ {partner company}
>
> Hi both — connecting you as promised.
>
> {Seller first name} sells on Amazon ({marketplace, product category}) and is
> looking for prep services in {region}.
>
> {Partner contact first name} runs {partner company} in {city, state} —
> they're a Daniks.AI partner center handling {services}.
>
> I'll leave you to it. {Seller first name}: mention Daniks.AI and they'll
> know where you're coming from.
>
> {sender}
