## Notes for the person building it

- One RSA per ad group; pin nothing at first, prune "Low"-rated assets after 2 weeks.
- Lesson from JP: exact keyword phrases inside headlines lift Ad Strength, but here that would mean competitor trademarks in copy — **accept "Average/Poor" Ad Strength**, it's a rating not a gate.
- Lesson from JP: the campaign wizard once wiped an ad group's content mid-build — after any wizard hiccup, re-check keywords + RSA are still there.
- Expect many "Low search volume" keyword flags (long-tails measured at 0) — leave them; they activate if volume appears. The phrase-match brand cores (pacvue, teikametrics, sellozo, adtomic…) carry the traffic.
- Week 1 search-term watch: bare `pacvue` (navigational logins → add negatives), `m19 amazon` (MTG card set!), `quartile *` (statistics), `scale insights` (generic BI queries).
