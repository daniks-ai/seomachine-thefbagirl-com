## Notes for the person building it

- One RSA per ad group; pin nothing at first — let assets rotate, then pin winners.
- JP lesson: aim for exact keyword phrases inside headlines (JP got "Poor" ad strength where the literal key phrase was missing). Each group's headline #1 mirrors its head keyword.
- `amazon werbung` (1,900/mo, mixed intent) rides in group 2 at a low bid — watch its search terms first; kill fast if buyer-intent queries leak through.
- After 2 weeks, prune headlines marked "Low" in the asset report and add 3–5 fresh ones.
- Passkey (Verify it's you) required for publish + budget edits — user does those steps on their device.
