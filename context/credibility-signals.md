## Claims we do NOT make (and why)

- ❌ "7-figure seller" — not independently verifiable, reads as guru bait.
- ❌ "Helped thousands of sellers" — unverifiable, and it's the blog's readers, not a customer base.
- ❌ "Featured in [big publication]" — only write this if it actually happens, with a link.
- ❌ "Certified Amazon expert" — no such certification exists; Amazon doesn't issue one.
- ❌ "Proven system" / "Repeatable formula" — reserved for guru content.
