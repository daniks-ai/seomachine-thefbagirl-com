## Claims we do NOT make (and why)

- ❌ "7-figure seller" — not independently verifiable, reads as guru bait.
- ❌ "Helped thousands of sellers" — unverifiable. Use "Daniks.AI powers hundreds of Amazon brands" only, which is specific and attributable to the software, not the blog.
- ❌ "Featured in [big publication]" — only write this if it actually happens, with a link.
- ❌ "Certified Amazon expert" — no such certification exists; Amazon does not issue one.
- ❌ "Proven system" / "Repeatable formula" — reserved for guru content.
- ❌ "10+ years selling on Amazon" — the real number is since 2018. The pre-Amazon career was in IT, not in Amazon. Do not collapse the two into a single "10+ years" claim.

## The framing mistake to avoid

Never write about Daniks.AI as if it were a SaaS Ekaterina is marketing. The correct framing is always: **seller-built, dogfooded on her own Top-1 Germany / Top-20 USA brand, and only then extended to other sellers.** Getting this ordering wrong turns her from a credible operator into a tool vendor in disguise — exactly the pattern readers are tired of.
