## Internal Linking Best Practices for TheFBAGirl

1. **Link Naturally**: Only link when genuinely helpful to the reader. A forced link kills trust.
2. **Vary Anchor Text**: Don't use "Amazon PPC strategy" every time — rotate between "my PPC guide," "the three-phase campaign structure," "Sponsored Products framework."
3. **3–5 Links Per Post**: Minimum 3 internal links; 4–5 is optimal. Pillar posts can carry up to 7.
4. **Deep Link, Don't Homepage**: Link to the specific blog/tutorial/review — never the homepage except in specific contexts.
5. **Early Links Matter**: At least one internal link in the first 500 words.
6. **Cross-Collection Linking**: Blog → Tutorial, Tutorial → Review, News → Blog. These create topical authority clusters.
7. **YouTube Video Embed**: If the source post has a `youtubeVideoId`, embed or link the video as well — not a replacement for internal links.
8. **Update on Publish**: Add new posts to this map as they're published so future posts can link back.
